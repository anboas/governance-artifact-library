 Statutory Notes and Related Subsidiaries 
 
 
 

 Effective Date of Repeal 

 Repeal effective Oct. 1, 1997, see section 727(b) of Pub. L. 104&ndash;201, set out in an Inclusion of Certain Designated Providers in Uniformed Services Health Care Delivery System note under section 1073 of Title 10 , Armed Forces.
