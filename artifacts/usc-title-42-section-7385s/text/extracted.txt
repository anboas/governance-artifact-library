 &sect;7385s. Definitions 
 
 

 In this part: 

 

 (1) The term "covered DOE contractor employee" means any Department of Energy contractor employee determined under section 7385s&ndash;4 of this title to have contracted a covered illness through exposure at a Department of Energy facility. 

 

 (2) The term "covered illness" means an illness or death resulting from exposure to a toxic substance. 

 

 (3) The term "Secretary" means the Secretary of Labor.
