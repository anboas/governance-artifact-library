 Statutory Notes and Related Subsidiaries

 , as amended by

 , which required development and implementation of a portfolio management framework for the National Nuclear Security Administration, was repealed by 
 Pub. L. 119&ndash;60, div. C, title XXXI, &sect;3111(b)(10), Dec. 18, 2025, 139 Stat. 1459 
 . See section 6112 of Title 10 , Armed Forces. 

 Nuclear Warhead Design Competition 

 
 
 Pub. L. 115&ndash;91, div. C, title XXXI, &sect;3118, Dec. 12, 2017, 131 Stat. 1890 
 , required the Administrator for Nuclear Security, in coordination with the Chairman of the Nuclear Weapons Council, to carry out a design competition for a nuclear warhead that could be employed on ballistic missiles of the United States by 2030, with a plan for the competition to be developed during fiscal year 2018 and implemented during fiscal year 2019. 

 Plan for Developing Exascale Computing and Incorporating Such Computing Into the Stockpile Stewardship Program 

 
 
 Pub. L. 113&ndash;66, div. C, title XXXI, &sect;3129, Dec. 26, 2013, 127 Stat. 1066 
 , which required development of a plan to develop exascale computing and incorporate such computing into the stockpile stewardship program, was transferred and is set out as a note under section 6111 of Title 10 , Armed Forces. 

 Report on the Plan for the Nuclear Weapons Stockpile, Nuclear Weapons Complex, and Delivery Platforms and Sense of Congress on Follow-on Negotiations to START Treaty 

 
 
 Pub. L. 111&ndash;84, div. A, title XII, &sect;1251, Oct. 28, 2009, 123 Stat. 2549 
 , which was set out as a note under section 2523 of this title and required a report on the plan for the nuclear weapons stockpile, nuclear weapons complex, and delivery platforms, was transferred and is set out as a note under section 6114 of Title 10 , Armed Forces.
