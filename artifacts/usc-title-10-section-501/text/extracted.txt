 &sect;501. Definition 
 
 

 In this chapter "enlistment" means original enlistment or reenlistment. 
 

 
 

 (Added 
 Pub. L. 90&ndash;235, &sect;2(a)(1)(B), Jan. 2, 1968, 81 Stat. 753 
 .)
