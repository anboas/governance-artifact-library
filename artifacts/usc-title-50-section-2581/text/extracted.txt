 &sect;&sect;2581 to 2582a. Repealed.

 Section 2581, Pub. L. 107&ndash;314, div. D, title XLIV, &sect;4401, formerly 
 Pub. L. 102&ndash;190, div. C, title XXXI, &sect;3134, Dec. 5, 1991, 105 Stat. 1575 
 ; renumbered Pub. L. 107&ndash;314, div. D, title XLIV, &sect;4401, by

 ; 
 Pub. L. 113&ndash;66, div. C, title XXXI, &sect;3146(e)(1), Dec. 26, 2013, 127 Stat. 1075 
 , established the Defense Environmental Cleanup Account. See section 6171 of Title 10 , Armed Forces. 

 Section 2582, Pub. L. 107&ndash;314, div. D, title XLIV, &sect;4402, formerly 
 Pub. L. 104&ndash;201, div. C, title XXXI, &sect;3153, Sept. 23, 1996, 110 Stat. 2839 
 ; renumbered Pub. L. 107&ndash;314, div. D, title XLIV, &sect;4402, and amended 
 Pub. L. 108&ndash;136, div. C, title XXXI, &sect;3141(g)(3), Nov. 24, 2003, 117 Stat. 1764 
 ; 
 Pub. L. 113&ndash;66, div. C, title XXXI, &sect;3146(e)(2), Dec. 26, 2013, 127 Stat. 1076 
 , related to development of future use plans for defense environmental cleanup. See section 6173 of Title 10 , Armed Forces. 

 Section 2582a, Pub. L. 107&ndash;314, div. D, title XLIV, &sect;4402A, as added 
 Pub. L. 111&ndash;383, div. C, title XXXI, &sect;3116(a), Jan. 7, 2011, 124 Stat. 4512 
 ; amended 
 Pub. L. 113&ndash;66, div. C, title XXXI, &sect;3146(e)(3), Dec. 26, 2013, 127 Stat. 1076 
 ; 
 Pub. L. 116&ndash;283, div. C, title XXXI, &sect;3122, Jan. 1, 2021, 134 Stat. 4381 
 , required annual submission of a future-years defense environmental cleanup plan. See section 6174 of Title 10 , Armed Forces.
