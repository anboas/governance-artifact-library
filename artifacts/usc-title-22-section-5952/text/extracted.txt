 , as amended by

 , which authorized the Secretary of Defense, through Dec. 31, 2018, to enter into agreements with any person (including a foreign government, international organization, multinational entity, or any other entity) under which the person would contribute funds for activities conducted under the Cooperative Threat Reduction Program, was repealed by

 . 

 Limited Waiver of Restrictions on Use of Funds for Threat Reduction in States of the Former Soviet Union 

 
 
 Pub. L. 107&ndash;314, div. A, title XIII, &sect;1306(a)&ndash;(d), Dec. 2, 2002, 116 Stat. 2673 
 , as amended by 
 Pub. L. 109&ndash;163, div. A, title XIII, &sect;1303(1), Jan. 6, 2006, 119 Stat. 3474 
 , which authorized the President to waive the restrictions and eligibility requirements set out in former section 5952(d) of this title for the obligation and expenditure of funds under that section for assistance to an independent state of the former Soviet Union, was repealed by 
 Pub. L. 113&ndash;291, div. A, title XIII, &sect;1351(10), Dec. 19, 2014, 128 Stat. 3607 
 . 

 Requirement To Consider Use of Revenue Generated by Activities Carried Out Under Cooperative Threat Reduction Programs

 Pub. L. 113&ndash;291, div. A, title XIII, &sect;1351(9), Dec. 19, 2014, 128 Stat. 3607 
 .
